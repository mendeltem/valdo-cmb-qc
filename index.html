Cerebral microbleed detection on VALDO 2021

Quality control of our two-stage microbleed detector on the 57 cross-validation cases of the VALDO 2021 challenge, Task 2 (T2*-weighted MRI). Every case is shown with the models that never saw it. Research and teaching use only; not a medical device.

Found, missed, extra NEW

Overview statistics by size class and site, then every correct, missed and false microbleed as a tile with axial, coronal, sagittal and 3D view. Filter by category and size, rate each finding yourself and download your answers as Excel.

Slice viewer

Two large axial images per case, reference on the left, the stage-1 U-Net on the right (two-seed ensemble with the CSF rule, without stage 2) coloured by verdict: green detected, yellow false alarm, red missed. Mouse wheel = slice, lossless PNG images.
